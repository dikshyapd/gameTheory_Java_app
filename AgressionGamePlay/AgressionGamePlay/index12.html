Agression Game Helper

This is a program that will help you play the game of agression in a stretegic way. The game will display messages that will help you to make the best decisions and to win the game as you play. The areas in this game are colored in terms of safety, so you can see where you are safe and where you are not. Green is safe, yellow is not safe, and red is very dangerous.

How to Play

Basic Rules:

  • • Players take turns placing armies on the linear battlefield
  • • Click on a cell and enter the number of armies to deploy
  • • You can only place armies in empty cells or cells you control
  • • Adjacent territories can be attacked during your turn

Victory Conditions:

  • • Control the majority of territories on the board
  • • Eliminate your opponent's armies completely
  • • Strategic positioning is key to victory
  • • Plan your moves carefully and outmaneuver your opponent

Game of Aggression

A strategic turn-based game where players compete to control territories on a linear battlefield. Deploy your armies wisely and outmaneuver your opponent to claim victory.
